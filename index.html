About

Divyadharshini is an information technology student who learns by building complete applications — from the database schema to the last pixel of the interface. She has developed four full-stack projects, each aimed at a real workflow rather than a tutorial exercise.

Her work focuses on practical, role-based software: dashboards, records, and booking flows that non-technical users can pick up without training. She pays attention to the parts that decide whether a tool gets used — secure sign-in, clean data entry, and screens that stay readable on any device.

She is currently preparing for campus placements and internships, sharpening her problem-solving and aptitude skills alongside her coursework.
